[00:00] Welcome to the show. Today we're talking about product-market fit and how Superhuman
measures it using a simple survey.

[03:15] The PMF survey asks users one question: how would you feel if you could no longer use
this product? Very disappointed, somewhat disappointed, or not disappointed. If 40% or more say
very disappointed, you likely have product-market fit.

[08:40] To improve retention, we segmented our very disappointed users and asked what they loved
most, then doubled down on that feature set for the broader user base. This is how you turn a
qualitative signal into a growth roadmap.

[15:02] A common growth mistake is chasing top-of-funnel acquisition before nailing retention.
Retention compounds; acquisition without retention just fills a leaky bucket.
